Privacy Policy

Effective: September 14, 2026

Pounce: Cat Metronome ("Pounce", "the app") is built to work entirely on your device. This policy explains what data the app touches, why, and — just as important — everything it does not do.

The short version

  • No account or sign-up is required to use the app.
  • Your practice history, streaks, and settings are stored only on your device.
  • The app contains no analytics, advertising, or tracking software of any kind.
  • Nothing you do in the app is sold or shared with data brokers.

Data stored on your device

Pounce saves the following locally, using standard iOS app storage:

  • Metronome and practice settings (tempo, time signature, sound preferences).
  • Practice history: session lengths, streaks, personal bests, and skill progress.
  • Reminder preferences (which reminders are on, and the times or conditions you chose).

None of this leaves your device. It is not transmitted to us or to any server — there simply isn't one. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Location (optional)

If you turn on "place" reminders in Settings, the app stores the coordinates of one location you choose — for example, wherever you usually practice — directly on your device. It uses this only to notice, on-device, when you're near that spot, so it can nudge you to practice. That coordinate is never transmitted anywhere, never leaves your device, and is deleted if you turn the reminder off or delete the app. If your device grants only "While Using the App" access, the reminder still works when you open the app near that place; background nudges require "Always" access, which you can grant or revoke at any time in iOS Settings.

Notifications

Reminder notifications are scheduled locally on your device using Apple's notification system. Pounce does not use push notifications and has no notification server — nothing about your schedule or habits is sent to us.

In-app purchases

Pounce offers a one-time "Pro" unlock via Apple's In-App Purchase system. Purchases are processed by Apple and by our payments partner, RevenueCat, which manages purchase validation and entitlement status. RevenueCat receives an anonymous, app-specific identifier and your purchase/transaction information in order to do this — it does not receive your name, contact details, or any of the practice data described above. See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy and Apple's Privacy Policy for details on how each of them handles data.

What we don't do

  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.
  • No advertising or ad networks.
  • No third-party tracking, and no Advertising Identifier (IDFA) use.
  • No accounts, so no passwords or personal profiles to store.

Children's privacy

Pounce is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone. Because the app doesn't collect personal information in the first place, this applies equally to users of any age.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the effective date above and post the revised version at this same address.
